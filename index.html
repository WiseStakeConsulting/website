How Earn As You Live works

Three steps. The protocol does the enforcing. You never send us a dollar.

1. Your people stake from their own wallets

They use our link and create their own staking position. Their wallet. Their money. Their keys. The minimum term is a year. We never touch anybody.s funds - not once, not ever.

2. They earn everything, from day one

Everything they are supposed to earn, they earn starting day one. Nothing sits in a waiting room and nothing waits on us.

The free program is the on-ramp, not the trap. When our PPS-1 standard goes live, your stakes can point at your own program instead of ours.
