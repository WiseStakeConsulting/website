No jargon

What is a stake? Think of a fruit tree.

You plant a seed, and the tree grows in your yard - not ours. For up to 42 years it bears fruit. Everything it grows is yours, and when the tree's life ends, its full value comes back to you too.

You can pick some fruit along the way, but every early harvest lowers what the tree is worth at the end. That is a stake: money you place once, that stays yours while it produces, and comes back to you at the end.

And the one we work with is a special tree. You can give it to somebody else, and it grows in their yard just the same. You can even split it - share a slice of everything it produces with a third person, for the life of the tree. That last part is what lets a gallery, a charity, or a dealership put their people's stakes to work without ever holding anybody's money.

The analogy stops where promises begin: the tree describes how the protocol works, not what it will pay. Nothing on this site is a guarantee.

Before anything else: get a wallet

Even if you pay us by card, your stake lives in a wallet - not in an account at a company. A wallet is a program on your own device that holds your money and your keys. Only you can move your funds. Not a bank. Not us. Nobody.

MetaMask

The one most people start with. Simple, widely supported, works in your browser and phone. metamask.io

Rabby

Built for people who end up on more than one network, and it checks every transaction before you sign it. A favorite once you get comfortable. rabby.io
