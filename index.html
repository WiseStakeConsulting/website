The free path

How the free rail works

Three steps. The protocol does the enforcing; you never send us a dollar.

1. Your people stake from their own wallets

Through our referral link, each member creates their own staking position - their wallet, their custody, their capital. Minimum term: 365 days. We never hold anyone's funds. Ever.

2. They earn everything, from day one

Full staking benefits, protocol distributions, and the referral share bonus accrue to your stakers immediately. Nothing waits on anyone's threshold.

3. Then graduate to your own rail

Our free rail is the starting line. When the PPS-1 standard goes live, your community's stakes can pin to your own accumulating splitter - your qualification builds from day one, with an agreed-upon split fixed for the life of every stake. Graduate with our help, on terms set in advance.

The Stakeholders movement

Subscriptions ask people to pay forever and own nothing. Stake-backed memberships invert that: members position once, keep custody end to end, and their capital returns at term's end - while the benefit, the access, the art, or the membership rides on a productive position instead of a billing cycle.

About

Built in Indianapolis

Wise Stake Consulting, LLC is an Indiana limited liability company founded on a simple observation: the businesses people love most - galleries, charities, dealerships, creators - are all still renting their relationships through monthly billing. We design the alternative - and we give the entry-level structure away, because a standard only matters if people can use it.

The company's heritage runs through the Wise token community, where stake-backed patronage was first lived rather than theorized. The owl in our mark is a nod to that lineage.
