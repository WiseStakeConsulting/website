Who controls the money

Your money. Your keys. Your call.

There is no "deposit account" here and no one to request a withdrawal from. Your funds never sit with a company - they live in a position you own, controlled by your wallet's key, on public contracts anyone can audit.

No admin at the platform can freeze, move, or touch your position. Not Wise Soft, not Wise Stake Consulting, not anyone. We can't see your seed phrase, can't sign for you, can't reach in. Placing funds is a transaction only your wallet can make. Exiting - even when it means a buyer takes over your position - is a transaction only your wallet can start. The one way anyone loses control of these funds is losing or sharing their own key - which is why the seed phrase rules in step 1 matter more than any password you've ever been told to protect.

Questions everyone asks

Straight answers.

Is my deposit guaranteed?

Honest split: your deposit is USDC, a dollar stablecoin - your principal does not swing with token prices. That is the strongpoint: the 40% pays on stable money. It is not risk-free: USDC is built to hold $1 but is issued by a company and has wobbled before; the yield depends on the platform reward buffer and real asset revenue; exits can have a queue; and the contracts, while exact, are still software. Stable describes your deposit, not the future. Do your own research, and never deposit money you need back on a schedule.

Do you hold my money?

No. Wise Stake Consulting never touches your funds. You deposit from your own wallet into the protocol. We earn referral commission from the platform on deposits made through our link - that's our disclosure, up front.

What happens after the pool caps?

Existing positions keep earning their locked rate for the rest of the window. New entries close. The platform team has stated the next pool will open at a different rate with higher node prices - their projection is ~60% APR, potentially three digits as the network grows. Nothing is set in stone until the capital lands.
