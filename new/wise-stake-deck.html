The reframe

Twelve bills a year.

A bill is money that leaves and never comes back. A stake is money that stays yours and works.

1 - The stake

Money placed once. It stays yours.

PLACE ONCE

One action. No recurring payment. Ever.

IT PRODUCES

Yield accrues to the position over its term.

IT RETURNS

Principal + production come back at term.

Described, never promised: what the tokens are worth later is the market's answer.

2 - Transferable

Give it. Sell it. Borrow against it.

The position changes hands mid-term, intact. Same term, same yield, same clock. Ownership moves - nothing restarts.
