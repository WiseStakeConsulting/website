The workflow

Four steps. One transaction to finish it.

1

Pick the category

What is this bundle for? A song, a family, a tithe, a membership. The category is permanent, on-chain, and keys the receipt's art.

2

Fund it

Back the bundle with a vault position (like a World Mobile wtnUSDC deposit), a fresh USDC deposit, a Signature card, or a brand-new stake.

3

Set the beneficiaries

Up to 6 wallets, each with a share of the daily interest. Splits are set at creation and permanent - the bundle pays them on every scrape, forever.

4

Wrap it

One signature from your wallet. You get a receipt NFT that controls the bundle - scrape it, close it, or hold it. The interest splits automatically.
