The Patronage Bundle Standard

One WISE Signature Stake. One membership or art NFT. One on-chain entitlement record binding them — with yield flowing to a patron, on the receiving side of interest.

Live on Ethereum Mainnet Fork-Validated Self-Funded Security Standing Bug Bounty

Abstract

PBS-1 is a standard for patronage bundles: a smart-contract wrapper that holds one WISE Signature Stake NFT together with one membership or art NFT (a CryptoKitty, a piece from an artist, a pass), and records — permanently, on-chain — who the patron is and what share of the stake's yield they receive.

The stake keeps earning WISE interest exactly as it always would. The wrapper adds an entitlement record and a fair split. Nothing more, nothing less. The principal is never touched, never pledged, and never put at risk by the protocol.

The idea in one sentence

A 2017 CryptoKitty can ride a 2026 yield engine — and the artist, the patron, or the partner you name gets paid every time the interest is scraped.

Lifecycle

1 · Create

The owner approves the wrapper for their stake NFT and their membership NFT, then calls createBundle, naming the patron and the split. Both NFTs move into the wrapper's custody and the entitlement record is written in the same transaction.

2 · Scrape

Owner or patron calls scrape(bundleId, scrapeDays) at any time — mid-term, day one, day 300. The wrapper commands the WISE engine's scrapeInterest, measures exactly what arrived, and pays the patron share and owner share instantly. The wrapper retains nothing. The principal never moves.

3 · Close

The owner may close a bundle: both NFTs return to the chosen address, the record deactivates. The same stake-and-NFT pair may be bundled together again — but an NFT can never be re-bound to a different stake. The pair is permanent by design.

Guarantees

  • Principal is sacred. The protocol only ever routes interest. The staked WISE stays staked for its full term.
  • Claims are early, not gated. Scraping works from day one of the term. Nobody waits for maturity to collect.
  • Either party can collect. A patron is never dependent on the owner to trigger payment.
  • The wrapper keeps nothing. Verified in testing: balance returns to zero after every scrape.
  • Binding is permanent. A bundled NFT can never attach to a different stake — provenance is untainted.
  • On-chain, not metadata. The entitlement record is the proof. Descriptions describe; the chain decides.

Security: The Warchest

PBS-1 secures itself the same way it pays its patrons — from interest, not from fees. The Warchest is a designated Patronage Bundle whose entire yield is paid out to anyone who finds a vulnerability in the protocol. If you can break it, the protocol thanks you in WISE.

Why a bounty instead of (only) an audit

An audit is two to four professionals for a fixed window; a standing bounty is every security researcher on earth, all the time, paid only for results. The Warchest starts small and grows with the protocol — and until it matures, exposure is capped by design: real stakes are limited to small, designated amounts. Independent third-party audit reviews may still be commissioned as the Warchest grows.

  • Yield is variable. Scraped amounts depend on the WISE engine's live global state. Nothing here is a promise of returns.
  • Bundles never pledge collateral. PBS-1 uses no lending path; the "no scraping while pledged" rule of Signature Stake LOCs does not apply.
  • Legacy quirks are handled. CryptoKitties predates modern NFT standards — the wrapper compensates for its approval behavior, and non-sequential stake IDs are read, never assumed.

Any ERC-721 Can Ride the Stake

The protocol does not care what an NFT means — it holds the token and routes the yield. Any ERC-721 NFT can be bundled with a WISE Signature Stake:

  • Art NFTs came first. Bundle #0001 pairs a 2017 CryptoKitty with a live stake — heritage art put to work.
  • ENS names are themselves ERC-721 tokens; an identity can hold its own yield.
  • Music NFTs let an artist ride the stake as patron — patronage with no label in the middle.
  • Memberships and passes turn the pattern into a service: the stake funds the benefit behind the token.
  • Tokenized real-world assets — property rights via legal wrappers, for example — follow the same shape: a unique claim, backed by what stands behind it.

The standard is the same for all of them: one unique token, one stake, one entitlement record. What changes is the story the token carries. Each new collection is fork-tested against the wrapper before it ships — the same validation Bundle #0001 went through.

The principle

An NFT is a unique claim. The value is what stands behind the claim. PBS-1 puts a yield engine behind it.

Questions

Do we have to wait for the stake term to end to claim?

No. Interest accrues from day one and can be scraped any day of the term. The permanent binding restricts which stake an NFT rides — never when you collect.

Can the patron take the stake or the NFT?

No. The patron's power is exactly one thing: trigger a scrape and receive their recorded share. Custody stays with the wrapper until the owner closes the bundle.

What happens at the end of the stake's term?

The owner closes the bundle (both NFTs come home) and the stake NFT can be burned for its principal plus final interest — exactly as if it had never been bundled.

What happens if someone finds a bug?

They get paid. The Warchest pays bounties from its own yield at published, automatic rates — Critical 50% of the pot down to Low 5% — with a written safe harbor for responsible reports. Security here is funded the same way everything else is: from interest.

Can a bundle have a different split than 50/50?

Yes. The split is chosen per bundle at creation, in basis points. Bundle #0001 is 50/50 as a statement of equal partnership, not a protocol limit.
