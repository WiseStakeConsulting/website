Signature Staking

One stake. Many lives.
All on the receiving side.

A Signature Stake is a position you create once, from your own wallet. It stays yours the whole time, earns from day one, and returns to you at the end of its term. It can be transferred, and you can share a slice of its yield with someone else for its life. No company holds it. No fee from us — we earn referral credit, disclosed up front.
