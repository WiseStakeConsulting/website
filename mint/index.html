Mint Your Art

You own your art. You always will. This page gives your artwork a permanent, timestamped record that you created it - stored so nobody can ever alter or remove it, not even us. The art is minted from your own wallet, so the creator record is yours from the very first block.

One honest note: this record proves who created it and when. It can't see the physical piece itself - that's exactly what the checklist below is for.

Step 1 - Capture your piece (do this on the call with us)

The full piece, straight-on Daylight from a window (not direct sun), no flash, fill the whole frame. Phone camera is perfect.
Detail shot of your signature Get close. Signatures and dates are what make a piece traceably yours.
Detail shot of the texture Angle the light so brushstrokes or texture show. Forgers fake a photo - they don't fake your brushwork.
A short video (10-20 seconds) Pan across the piece slowly, then show the back. Video is very hard to fake and stores permanently.
You, holding the piece Simple, human, and the single best proof that the piece was in your hands at minting time.

Step 3 - Upload the files (from YOUR device)

Your files go straight from your device into permanent decentralized storage. They never pass through our hands. Uploads use IPFS - a storage network where files live across many computers, so no single company (including ours) can take them down.
