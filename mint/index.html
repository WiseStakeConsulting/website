Cards That Give

One sale. Two gifts.

A cause you love, built the Wise Stake way: not a fundraiser that ends, a card that keeps giving.

  • One-time proceeds: the card sells once — the sale proceeds go to the cause, in full, on-chain and visible to anyone.
  • Optional yield share: if the card is bundled with a stake, a share of the daily interest it earns can flow to the same cause for the life of the bundle.
  • Your name on it, or not: give publicly or anonymously — the record shows what you choose.

If you have a cause — a church, a school, a family, a foundation — talk to us and we'll design the card with you.

How a giving card works

Minted from the giver's wallet. Sold once, proceeds sent to the cause's wallet. If bundled, the yield share flows daily. The giver chooses the cause, the split, and the story on the card.

We're careful with promises here: structures like this can have tax and legal implications, and we'll point you to qualified counsel before anything is final.

Selling your work

We don't run auctions — yet.

When you're ready to sell or auction your minted piece, it lists on the established marketplaces that already connect to your wallet. You set the price, you keep the sale. We take no cut of what you sell — our fee is the flat mint fee, and that's the whole bill. When a Wise Stake auction option goes live, it will be announced here, never before it's real.
